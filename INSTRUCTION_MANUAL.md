## Getting Started

### Accessing the Platform

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the Hibiscus StudyPal website
2. You will see the colorful login page with the Hibiscus StudyPal logo

### Creating an Account

**For Students:**
1. On the login page, ensure "Student" mode is selected (default)
2. Click the "Register" tab
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Username (this will be your login ID)
   - Password
   - Grade (optional)
4. Click "Create Account"

**For Teachers:**
1. On the login page, click "Teacher" button
2. Click the "Register" tab
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Username
   - Password
4. Click "Create Teacher Account"

### Logging In

1. Select your role (Student, Teacher, or Admin)
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click "Sign In"

## For Students

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see your personal dashboard with:
- **Stats Overview**: Your XP points, current level, study streak, and total study time
- **Quick Actions**: Easy access to common tasks
- **Recent Activity**: Your latest study sessions and achievements

### Joining a Class

1. Your teacher will provide you with a **Class Code** (6 characters, e.g., "ABC123")
2. From your dashboard or Classes page, click "Join Class"
3. Enter the class code provided by your teacher
4. Click "Join" - you'll now have access to all class materials

### Viewing Class Materials

Once you've joined a class, you can access:

**Assignments Tab**
- View all assignments posted by your teacher
- See due dates and descriptions
- Track which assignments you've completed

**To-Dos Tab**
- Daily tasks and reminders from your teacher
- Organized by date for easy planning

**Exams Tab**
- Upcoming exam dates and details
- Downloadable study materials and past papers
- Exam schedules and important dates

**Resources Tab**
- Study materials shared by your teacher
- Links to external resources
- Downloadable files (PDFs, documents, etc.)

**Quizzes Tab**
- Take quizzes created by your teacher
- View your scores and feedback
- Track your quiz history

### Taking a Quiz

1. Go to the Quizzes tab in your class
2. Click on an available quiz
3. Read the instructions and time limit (if any)
4. Answer all questions:
   - **Multiple Choice (MCQ)**: Select one correct answer
   - **Multiple Select**: Select all correct answers
   - **Short Answer**: Type your response
   - **Essay**: Write a detailed response
5. Click "Submit Quiz" when finished
6. View your score immediately for auto-graded questions

### Study Tools

**Notes**
- Create and organize study notes
- Search through your notes
- Categorize by subject

**Flashcards**
- Create flashcard decks for memorization
- Uses spaced repetition for optimal learning
- Rate your recall to improve future sessions

**AI Tutor**
- Ask questions and get instant explanations
- Get help with homework problems
- Explore topics in depth

**Pomodoro Timer**
- Focus sessions with timed study intervals
- Track your productive time
- Customizable session lengths

**Study Groups**
- Create study groups and get a unique **Group Code** (6 characters)
- Share your group code with friends so they can join
- Click the code on your group card to copy it
- Use "Join by Code" button to enter a code from a friend
- Collaborate with classmates in group discussions

### Gamification & Progress

**XP Points**
- Earn XP for completing study activities
- Level up as you accumulate points

**Streaks**
- Maintain daily study streaks
- Don't break your streak for bonus rewards

**Achievements**
- Unlock badges for milestones
- Track your accomplishments

**Analytics**
- View your study patterns
- Track progress over time
- Identify areas for improvement

## For Teachers

### Teacher Dashboard

After logging in, you'll access your teacher dashboard where you can manage all your classes.

### Creating a Class

1. Click "Create Class" button
2. Fill in the class details:
   - **Class Name**: e.g., "Biology 101 - Period 3"
   - **Subject**: The subject being taught
   - **Description**: Additional information about the class
   - **Grade Level**: The intended grade level
3. Click "Create"
4. A unique **Class Code** will be automatically generated

### Sharing the Class Code

- Each class has a unique 6-character code (e.g., "XYZ789")
- Share this code with your students so they can join
- The code is displayed at the top of your class page
- You can copy the code with one click

### Managing Students

**Viewing Enrolled Students**
1. Open your class
2. Click the "Students" tab
3. See all enrolled students with their names and join dates

**Adding Students Manually**
1. Go to the Students tab
2. Click "Add Student"
3. Enter the student's username
4. Click "Add" - the student will be enrolled immediately

**Removing Students**
1. Go to the Students tab
2. Find the student you want to remove
3. Click the remove button (X icon)
4. Confirm the removal

### Creating Class Content

**Assignments**
1. Go to the Assignments tab
2. Click "Add Assignment"
3. Fill in:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Due Date
4. Click "Save"

**To-Dos**
1. Go to the To-Dos tab
2. Click "Add To-Do"
3. Enter the task details and date
4. Click "Save"

**Exams**
1. Go to the Exams tab
2. Click "Add Exam"
3. Fill in exam details:
   - Title
   - Date
   - Description
   - Upload exam papers or study guides (optional)
4. Click "Save"

**Resources**
1. Go to the Resources tab
2. Click "Add Resource"
3. Choose type:
   - **Link**: Enter a URL
   - **Text Note**: Add text content
   - **File Upload**: Upload PDFs, documents, etc.
4. Add a title and description
5. Click "Save"

### Creating Quizzes (Quiz Builder)

The in-app quiz builder allows you to create comprehensive quizzes with multiple question types.

**Creating a New Quiz**

1. Go to the Quizzes tab
2. Click "Create Quiz"
3. Fill in quiz details:
   - **Title**: Name of the quiz
   - **Description**: Instructions or context
   - **Time Limit**: Optional time limit in minutes
   - **Passing Score**: Minimum percentage to pass
4. Click "Create"

**Adding Questions**

Click "Add Question" and choose a question type:

**Multiple Choice (MCQ)**
- Enter the question text
- Add answer options (A, B, C, D, etc.)
- Select the correct answer
- Set point value

**Multiple Select**
- Enter the question text
- Add answer options
- Select ALL correct answers
- Set point value

**Short Answer**
- Enter the question text
- Add accepted answers (multiple variations allowed)
- Set point value
- Auto-graded based on exact matches

**Essay**
- Enter the question text
- Add a grading rubric (optional)
- Set maximum word count (optional)
- Set point value
- Requires manual grading

**Managing Questions**
- Drag to reorder questions
- Edit questions by clicking on them
- Delete questions with the trash icon

**Publishing a Quiz**
1. Review all questions
2. Toggle "Published" to make it available to students
3. Students can only see and take published quizzes

### Grading Quizzes

**Auto-Grading**
- MCQ and Multiple Select questions are graded automatically
- Short Answer questions with exact matches are auto-graded
- Scores appear instantly for students

**Manual Grading**
1. Go to the Quizzes tab
2. Click on a quiz to view submissions
3. Click on a student's attempt
4. For essay questions:
   - Read the student's response
   - Enter points earned
   - Add feedback (optional)
5. Click "Save Grade"
6. The total score updates automatically

**Viewing Results**
- See all student submissions
- View individual scores
- Track class performance
- Export results if needed

## Troubleshooting & FAQ

### Common Issues

**Q: I forgot my password**
A: Contact your administrator to reset your password.

**Q: The class code isn't working**
A: Make sure you're entering the code exactly as given (codes are case-sensitive). Check with your teacher that the code is correct.

**Q: I can't see a quiz**
A: The quiz may not be published yet. Check with your teacher about availability.

**Q: My quiz submission didn't save**
A: Make sure you clicked "Submit Quiz" before the time ran out. Contact your teacher if you experience technical issues.

**Q: I accidentally left a class**
A: Ask your teacher for the class code and rejoin.

### For Teachers

**Q: How do I change a class code?**
A: Class codes are automatically generated and cannot be changed. If needed, you can create a new class.

**Q: Can I edit a quiz after publishing?**
A: Yes, you can unpublish, edit, and republish. Note that existing student attempts will remain.

**Q: How do I see which students completed assignments?**
A: Go to the Assignments tab and click on an assignment to see completion status.

### For Administrators

**Q: How do I add a new admin?**
A: Currently, admin accounts are created at the system level. Contact your technical administrator.

**Q: Can I delete all data for a student?**
A: Yes, removing a student from the platform will remove their associated data.
